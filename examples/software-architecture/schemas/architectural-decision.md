# Architectural Decision Record (ADR)

## Context
Describe the forces at play, including technical, business, and social factors.

## Decision
State the chosen approach clearly and unambiguously.

## Consequences
List the resulting context — both positive and negative outcomes of the decision.

## Template Usage
